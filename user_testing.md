# GestureSpeak AI – User Testing & Usability Evaluation

## Objective

To evaluate the live GestureSpeak AI system under practical webcam conditions and measure gesture recognition reliability, usability, and response behavior.

## Test Conditions

- Platform: Desktop/Laptop
- Input: Webcam
- Recognition: MediaPipe hand landmarks + Random Forest classifier
- Gesture Classes: 10
- Test Conditions:
  1. Normal lighting
  2. Different hand distances
  3. Slight hand-angle variations
