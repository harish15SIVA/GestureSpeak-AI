# GestureSpeak AI — Usability Study

## 1. Objective

The objective of this usability study is to evaluate the ease of use, clarity, accessibility, gesture recognition experience, and speech output of the GestureSpeak AI prototype.

---

## 2. Testing Method

Five participants were considered for a structured evaluation of the GestureSpeak AI prototype.

Each participant was asked to:

1. Open the GestureSpeak AI application.
2. Start the webcam.
3. View the Gesture Guide.
4. Perform the supported gestures.
5. Observe the recognized gesture and confidence score.
6. Check the generated communication text.
7. Test the optional speech output.
8. Provide feedback about usability and accessibility.

## 6. Key Observations

- The gesture guide provides clear instructions for the predefined gestures.
- The interface provides immediate visual feedback after gesture recognition.
- Text output makes the recognized communication intent easy to understand.
- Speech output provides an additional communication channel.
- Large text and accessibility controls improve readability.
- Recognition can be affected by hand position, distance, lighting, and camera angle.

---

## 7. User Feedback

The evaluation focused on the following areas:

- Simple and understandable interface
- Clear gesture instructions
- Immediate recognition feedback
- Useful text-to-speech functionality
- Need for improved robustness under different environmental conditions
- Potential for expanding the number of supported gestures

---

## 8. Observed Limitations

- Some gestures may require the user to hold the hand steadily.
- Similar hand configurations can occasionally produce incorrect predictions.
- Lighting and camera positioning can affect recognition.
- The current system supports a predefined vocabulary rather than complete sign-language interpretation.

---

## 9. Suggested Improvements

Based on the usability evaluation, future improvements include:

1. Increase dataset diversity.
2. Improve recognition under different lighting conditions.
3. Improve recognition for different hand angles and distances.
4. Expand the gesture vocabulary.
5. Conduct larger usability studies with accessibility stakeholders.
6. Improve real-world robustness and response consistency.

---

## 10. Conclusion

The usability evaluation indicates that GestureSpeak AI provides a simple interface for communicating predefined everyday needs through hand gestures.

The prototype combines gesture recognition, visual text output, optional speech synthesis, and accessibility features into a single communication workflow.

The evaluation also identifies opportunities to improve robustness, expand the gesture vocabulary, and conduct larger structured studies with target accessibility users.

> **Academic note:** Participant identities are anonymized. Results should be interpreted as prototype usability observations and not as a clinical or medical evaluation.
